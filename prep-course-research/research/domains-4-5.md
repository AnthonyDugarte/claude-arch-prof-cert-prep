## Claim discipline and limitations

- **Guide facts:** only the local guide establishes blueprint weights and objective wording. The modules do not claim official question provenance.
- **External facts:** Anthropic documentation establishes product behavior as of access date. NIST, HHS, European Commission, and FedRAMP establish their own frameworks and guidance. The course places citations next to these claims.
- **Architect recommendations:** suggested metric gates, experiment design, risk register fields, approval thresholds, and lab acceptance criteria are exercises in professional judgment. They are explicitly described as illustrative or recommended, not as source-mandated controls.
- **Unresolved:** No organization-specific BAA, DPA, FedRAMP certification package, data-flow diagram, legal opinion, or production telemetry was supplied. Therefore no statement can certify a real system's compliance, model safety rate, cost reduction, or fairness. Pricing, supported models, feature eligibility, and FedRAMP Marketplace status need checking again before deployment.
