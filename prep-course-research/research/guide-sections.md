## Interpretation conflicts to preserve during consolidation

1. P2 describes a Foundations-to-Professional progression, while G and indexed P4 indicate no mandatory prior certification. Curriculum progression is not a demonstrated exam prerequisite; follow the specific guide/policy for eligibility questions.
2. P2 restricts current exam access to Partner Network members. G's lack of mandatory coursework does not negate an access rule.
3. Public Academy course badges are not the proctored Professional credential. Foundations materials use a different blueprint and cannot replace G's seven domains.
4. The new public Academy catalog and Partner Academy pages are separate resource surfaces. A 403 from the research tool neither proves a paywall nor proves a learner cannot access the page.
5. Exam policy numbers are intentionally attributed to G. The search log exposes what remains unverified, rather than implying a successful search authenticated everything.
